02 / about

I'm studying Computer Technology at Bowie State University, with coursework spanning network design and administration, server administration, network security, database development, and programming in Python, Java, and C++. CTEC 450 with Prof. Carter is the design-heavy one: requirements, topology, addressing, segmentation, and defending the choices in writing.

Alongside school I work full shifts on a retail floor at Adidas, which is where the non-technical half of the job got built — reading what someone actually needs, working a crowded room with a team, and staying steady when it's busy. Most of what I know about troubleshooting came from breaking something on purpose and then reading the logs.
